Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

ForgeAI operates strictly as a client-side application. All your data, including your API keys, startup context, and chat history, is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. We do not transmit or store your personal data on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Since your data remains on your device, we do not use your information for analytics, tracking, or marketing. The only data processed is the data sent directly from your browser to the Google Gemini API using your provided API key.

3. Third-Party Services

When using ForgeAI, your prompts and context are sent directly to the Google Gemini API. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy to understand how they handle data transmitted through their API services.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@forgeai.co.
